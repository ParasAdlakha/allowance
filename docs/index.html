Give your AI agent an allowance, not your wallet.

Allowance lets a shopping agent spend real money through PayPal, but only inside a mandate you signed. The model proposes; a deterministic gate decides; PayPal pays, asks you, or nothing moves.

How it works

01 MANDATE
Write the rules oncePlain English → model drafts a policy → code reverts anything you didn't say → you sign and PayPal reserves the budget.
02 ERRAND
The agent shopsIt reads untrusted shop pages and can only propose a cart. It has no payment tool.
03 GATE
15 rules in plain codeRe-prices from the catalog; checks merchant, payee, quantity, subscriptions, budget, price drift.
04 PAY · ASK · AUDIT
Money moves, or you decidePartial capture against the hold, or approve in PayPal Checkout. Ledger, tracking, refunds.
The model drafts. Code checks. You sign.

Gemini kept loosening the rules.

Asked to compile "under $120 a month, ask me above $40, never more than 3 of one thing", it repeatedly allowed subscriptions. Allowance compares every draft against your words and reverts what you never granted.

⚠ Model allowed subscriptions; your text did not. Reverted.

Eval

Twelve errands, the same model (gemini-3.5-flash-lite) and the same shops, three agents. Dollars paid outside the signed mandate:

Toolkit, plain promptToolkit, hardened promptAllowance gate
Outside the mandate$127.60 3/12 errands$127.60 3/12 errands$0.00
Task success75%75%100%

The model resisted every planted instruction. It still paid a checkout price 33% above the page, broke a $60 per-order cap, and skipped an approval the owner asked for: arithmetic over prices it never sees. A better prompt changed nothing; code at the point of payment did. Raw results.
